I started this project when I was crippled in Penang. 
I figured, given that I wasn't able to go to the gym. 
I should do something to exercise my mind.

This started out as being something to build while I worked through a book on Java/Spring Boot 
that I'd won the year prior at an event in Vietnam. I worked through the book, initially, 
consistently, following the exercises... but I quickly became bored. I learn best by doing, and 
by building, and I wasn't building anything. I dealt with this fact by scrolling through 
Instagram, where I saw that some person or other had built a dating app to teach themselves 
C#, and it wound up making them a lot of money.

And then I thought, about what it was that was missing in this world, in regards to this notion. 
The fact is that Tinder, or Bumble, or whatever the fuck you use is effectively just a meat 
market, with an algorithm stacked in a way you do not quite know, but arguably without the 
design being geared towards you being happy. Sometimes it works, but when I'm swiping through 
people on the sole basis of their face, and swiping left on people who provide no such thing... 
It made me think back to a site I used as a teenager called Anonidate.

This was a website that I used back in my teenage years, with the premise being it was a dating 
site for people from 4chan, SomethingAwful, or wherever else people like to hang out on the internet. 
The gimmick was that all you got to know about a person was their general location, name, age, 
gender, bio, and what sites you frequent. There were no pictures, and it was generally quite 
simple, in terms of implementation. There were no ads, no monetizaiton, and the graphics were 
very early Web 2.0. Thing was, this site actually worked. I met a couple of people who would 
go on to have very profound impacts on my life, be they via dating, or friendship, and I met 
up with several people in my area.

The site was operational for... a good number of years, until at some point in the second 
decade of the 21st century, when it was shut down.

I'd noticed at the time, however it wasn't something that really came to mind until last year, 
finding myself crippled, and unable to either travel or exercise while I volunteered at a hostel 
in Penang, Malaysia in exchange for room & board, in a position where I had vast quantities of 
free time.

So, I decided to build it. The domain was registered (anonidate.com) on October 1, 2025, however 
work had been underway for a good amount of time.

I built out the app, utilizing a Claude subscription, and using the code, however writing it 
out by hand. This method improved literacy within the codebase, however in constrast to my later 
works, it came with a subsantial time penalty. It was faster than running through all of the 
logics by hand. I'd consider this system to be closer to hand-crafted, however it was the 
beginning of my feeling of being able to count on the outputs of an LLM for coding problems.



There is another element to this, in terms of the ways the scope crept. While I started out with 
the aim to produce a 1:1 replica, in terms of the original Anonidate, I wound up having the idea 
to implement an ability to meet-up with people for events... I expended a lot of energy thinking 
about it, thinking it such a fucking good idea... and it is. It's called nomadtable.

That's not my app, it's a direction I could have gone in. It was a potential vision, or idea, or 
direction, however it wasn't the direction I actually headed in. In fact, despite me having 
paid for the domain name, I never actually pushed the site to production. I had a money scare, 
right around this time, and my left-eye just wouldn't stop twitching. I wound up leaving where 
I was (Penang, Malaysia), and going to Bangkok in the pursuit of a girl, which set me off on a 
very different course, wherein my primary goal was to apply for jobs.

That's how the scope crept, and that's the way it crept BECAUSE I was looking to monetize 
this project. It was never monetized, originally. It simply was, as it was, and it did good 
for people without asking for anything back, and trying to apply startup logic to such a 
concept is fallacious... at the same time, when the purpse of the enterprise is decidedly 
without the premise of profit, it's much harder to focus upon, when your financial security 
is at threat... and it is.
